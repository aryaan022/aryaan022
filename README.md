<h2 align="center">🚀 Featured Engineering</h2>

### 🤖 AI Resume Analyzer & Interview Preparation Platform  *(Hero Project)*
> **An intelligent, automated career assistant** built to bridge the gap between job seekers and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). It comprehensively analyzes resumes against specific job descriptions, identifies critical skill gaps, and generates ATS-optimized documents. Furthermore, it dynamically creates personalized interview questions and assists candidates in rigorous technical interview preparation.
> 
> **Architecture & Features:** Resume Parsing & Upload • Deep JD Analysis • Real-time ATS Scoring • Skill Gap Detection • Automated Resume Improvement • AI Interview Question Generator • PDF Resume Export • JWT Token Blacklisting Auth
> 
> <b>Tech Stack:</b> <code>React</code> <code>Node.js</code> <code>Express.js</code> <code>MongoDB</code> <code>Gemini API</code> <code>JWT</code> <code>Puppeteer</code>
